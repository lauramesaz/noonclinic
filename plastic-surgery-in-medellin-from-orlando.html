    Plastic surgery in Medellin
    from Orlando and Tampa

    Flights, timing, recovery stay and safety checks for Central Florida patients considering surgery in Colombia.

    International patients · noon Clinic team · Updated October 2, 2026

    If you live in Orlando, Kissimmee or Tampa and you're researching plastic surgery in Medellin from Orlando, the first thing to sort out is the route. Central Florida has its own flight options, and they change with the season. This page covers flights from MCO and Tampa, timing around work and hurricane season, how long to stay in Medellín, Colombia, and the rules we follow at noon Clinic.

    Getting from Orlando or Tampa to Medellín

    Central Florida has better access than most people expect.

    • From Orlando (MCO): Avianca operates a seasonal nonstop to Medellín, and Frontier has announced a daily flight starting December 10, 2026. Schedules change, so confirm current dates before booking.
    • From Tampa (TPA): there's no nonstop to Medellín. Avianca flies nonstop to Bogotá, Colombia's capital, and you connect on a short domestic flight. Another option is to drive to MCO and fly direct.

    Medellín's international airport, José María Córdova (MDE), is actually in Rionegro, outside the city. Plan for the drive between the airport and where you're staying. It matters most on the day you fly home after surgery.

    Plastic surgery in Medellín from Orlando: how long to stay

    This is the part people underestimate. You don't fly in, have surgery and fly out the same week. Our reference ranges:

    • Face surgery: 1 to 2 weeks.
    • Breast surgery: 1 to 2 weeks.
    • Body contouring and combined procedures, such as liposuction, a tummy tuck or a mommy makeover: 2 to 3 weeks. (Procedure pages are in Spanish.)
    • Non-surgical aesthetic treatments: usually same day or a few days.

    For context, the American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) suggests waiting 5 to 7 days before flying after body procedures and 7 to 10 days after facial surgery. It also notes that the risk of blood clots from immobility, long flights included, is higher in the 6 weeks after surgery. At noon, your flight home isn't set by a chart. Your surgeon clears you at a follow-up visit, so book a flexible return ticket.

    Timing your trip from Central Florida

    If you work in hospitality or the theme parks, you already know summer and the holidays are the hardest time to get off. Start with how many consecutive days you can take, then see which procedure fits. Only have a week? Then a tummy tuck isn't the right plan for this trip, and we'd rather tell you that upfront.

    Weather on the return leg matters too. Florida summers are hot and humid, and summer is also hurricane season. Flying home in a compression garment into that heat is uncomfortable, and a storm can push a flight. Medellín itself has mild weather year-round, which helps during recovery.

    The time difference is barely noticeable. Colombia doesn't observe daylight saving time, so Orlando matches Medellín in winter and is one hour ahead from March to November. That also makes things easy once you're home: we check in remotely over WhatsApp or video call. It doesn't replace your in-person follow-up visits in Medellín before you fly, and for an emergency, go to an emergency room in Central Florida.

    Entry rules for US citizens

    About 84,000 people of Colombian origin live in the Orlando area and about 45,000 in the Tampa area, according to the US Census (ACS 2024). Kissimmee is one of the places where that community is most visible. Which passport you travel on matters. If you're a US citizen, you don't need a tourist visa for Colombia. Entry is granted for up to 90 days, extendable up to 180 per year, and Migración Colombia's rules include medical treatment as a permitted tourist activity. Migración Colombia also recommends completing the free online Check-MIG form.

    If you hold both US and Colombian citizenship, travel with your valid Colombian passport. Either way, check current requirements before you fly. We don't give immigration advice.

    Consultation and safety in Medellín

    We can talk on WhatsApp and book your appointment, but nobody is "approved" for surgery from a photo. Your surgeon examines you in person in Medellín and orders pre-op lab work. If your results aren't where they should be, surgery is postponed, even if you've already flown in. Surgery happens in licensed operating rooms at Q2, never in an office.

    Every surgery carries risks, including bleeding, infection, healing problems and anesthesia complications. We go through them one by one before you sign the informed consent. Our safety page (in Spanish) lists six warning signs worth checking at any clinic, ours included.

    Bring someone with you

    For surgery, you need an adult with you for the first several days. Patients with family in Colombia have this covered. If you don't, come with your partner, your mom or a close friend, and stay near the clinic so follow-up visits are easy.

    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a direct flight from Orlando to Medellin?

    Yes. Avianca runs a seasonal nonstop from Orlando (MCO) to Medellín, and Frontier has announced a daily flight starting December 10, 2026. Check current schedules before you book.

    Can I fly from Tampa to Medellin nonstop?

    No. From Tampa, Avianca flies nonstop to Bogotá, and from there you take a short domestic flight to Medellín. Another option is to drive to Orlando and fly direct.

    Do I need a visa to have surgery in Colombia as a US citizen?

    US citizens don't need a tourist visa. Entry is granted for up to 90 days, extendable, and Colombian rules list medical treatment as a permitted activity. Check current requirements before you travel.

    How long should I stay in Medellin after a tummy tuck or lipo?

    As a guide, body contouring and combined procedures need 2 to 3 weeks in the city. Your surgeon clears you to fly home at a follow-up visit, based on how you are healing.

    Can I get a consultation online before traveling?

    We can share information and book your appointment over WhatsApp, but the consultation itself happens in person in Medellín. No one is scheduled for surgery without being examined first.

    What kind of follow-up do I get after flying home to Orlando?

    Remote follow-up over WhatsApp or video call, so you can ask about your recovery. It's not a substitute for the in-person visits in Medellín before your surgeon clears you to fly. In an emergency, go to an emergency room wherever you are.
