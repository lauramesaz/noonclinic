    Lodging and recovery guide

    Where should you
    stay to recover in Medellin?

    This isn't booking a vacation rental. It's choosing where you'll actually heal.

    Guides · noon Clinic team · Updated October 5, 2026

    You've got twelve browser tabs open with apartments in Medellin and no idea which one to pick. Fair enough: nobody teaches you how to book lodging for this. It's not a vacation rental, and it's not a hotel for sightseeing: it's choosing where to stay in Medellin to recover after surgery, in the place your body most needs to be still. That one decision shapes how the whole recovery feels.

    Everything about the procedure itself (what it is, who it is for, recovery and risks) is on our Liposuction and liposculpture in Medellín page.

    Where to stay in Medellin: pick distance over the view

    The first thing to check isn't the balcony or the pool. It's the drive. The closer you stay to the clinic and to wherever your follow-up appointments happen, the simpler your recovery: fewer minutes in a car on the days sitting down, buckling a seatbelt, or getting in and out hurts more than usual.

    That doesn't mean picking the priciest or trendiest building you can find. It means asking for the address of your consultation and your follow-ups before you book, and looking nearby. A short ride beats a nice view, every time, in the first week.

    MobilityElevator or ground floor: no stairs to manage the first days

    What actually matters when you pick a place to sleep

    Forget the decor for a minute. Check three things: whether there's an elevator or the unit is on the ground floor, whether the bathroom is easy to use without straining your arms or your abdomen, and whether there's at least a small kitchen so you're not leaving to find food every time you're hungry. None of that shows up in most listing photos, so message the host before you pay.

    If you're coming from a city with a real winter, like Boston or New York, you'll notice how little you need to think about heating: Medellin has a mild climate nearly year-round, roughly 63 to 82 °F. The question here isn't the cold. It's how easy the space itself is to move around in.

    How long to book, and why you shouldn't lock in a return date

    As a reference, face and breast procedures run 1 to 2 weeks; body contouring and combined surgeries, 2 to 3; non-surgical treatments, often the same day or a few days. These are reference ranges, not a promise: the exact day you're cleared to leave comes from your surgeon at your follow-up visit, not the calendar you built from home months earlier.

    That's why a flexible cancellation policy is worth more than the lowest nightly rate with no changes allowed. The same goes for your return flight, as we cover in when can I fly after liposuction: a date that can move costs you less than a fixed one you have to break.

    Two people, not one

    If you're having surgery, you'll need an adult companion with you, at least for the first few days. That changes what you're booking: not a studio for one, but a space where two people can actually be comfortable for a week or two without getting in each other's way. Worth discussing with whoever's traveling with you before you book, not after you land.

    And here's a limit worth saying plainly: no apartment, however well located, replaces what gets decided at your in-person consultation. You can book the perfect place and still have your surgery date move if something in your labs calls for it. Lodging gets built around your medical process, not the other way around.

    What to do this week

    Look for options near where your consultation and follow-ups will happen, confirm there's an elevator or a ground-floor unit, and book with flexible cancellation. If you already have a date with us, or you're close to one, message us on WhatsApp before you pay: we'll confirm the expected length of stay for your procedure so you don't book too little, or too much.

    It's also worth reading what to pack for plastic surgery in Colombia and how long to stay in Colombia after plastic surgery. Between the three, most of the practical side of your trip is covered.

    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently asked questions

    Should I book a hotel or an apartment?

    Either works. What matters is an elevator or a ground-floor unit, enough space for a companion, and ideally a small kitchen so you don't have to go out for every meal the first days.

    How close to the clinic should I stay?

    As close as you reasonably can, to the clinic and to wherever your follow-up visits happen. Less time in a car matters most on the days moving around is hardest.

    Do I really need an elevator?

    If your place has stairs and no elevator, going up and down the first few days can be harder than it sounds from home. Check this before you book, not after you land.

    Can I change my checkout date if recovery takes longer?

    Book with a flexible cancellation policy, the same way you should book your flight home. Your surgeon clears you to leave at your follow-up, not the calendar you built from the US.

    Who stays with me the first few days?

    An adult companion, if you're having surgery. Book for two people, not one, even if you're traveling alone to Medellin.

    Can I book lodging before my in-person consultation?

    You can browse options early, but confirm your expected length of stay after your consultation, once your surgeon knows your case and procedure.
