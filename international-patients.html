    International patients

    How your trip works

    1. Message us before you buy ticketsTell us what you're interested in and where you're flying from. We get your details ready on WhatsApp.
    2. In-person consultation when you arriveYour specialist examines you in Medellín and, for surgery, orders your pre-op tests.
    3. Your procedureSurgery is always performed in an operating room, never in a doctor's office.
    4. Recovery and follow-upsYou stay in the city for the days your specialist recommends.
    5. Flying home and follow-upYou fly when your specialist clears you. Once you're home, we stay in touch by WhatsApp or video call.
