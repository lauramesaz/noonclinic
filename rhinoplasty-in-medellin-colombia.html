    Flying in for a rhinoplasty

    Rhinoplasty in Medellin,
    Colombia, for US Patients

    It's the one surgery on this list you can't cover with clothing. That changes how you should plan the weeks after it, not just the trip itself.

    Guides · noon Clinic team · Updated October 15, 2026

    You've been noticing the bump in every photo for years, angling your face away from the camera in group shots, and you already know exactly what you'd change. Here's what makes a rhinoplasty different from almost every other surgery on this list: you can't cover it with clothes or a compression garment. It's your face, the same one on every work video call and the one your family in Colombia sees the next time you visit. So before you look at flights to Medellín, it's worth understanding not just how long the surgery takes, but how long it takes to actually look different.

    What a rhinoplasty in Medellín does, and when it's paired with septoplasty

    Rhinoplasty changes the shape, size or proportion of the nose: it can correct a prominent bump, a tip that's wide, drooping or undefined, or aim for better balance with the rest of the face. When there's also trouble breathing because of the septum, it can be combined with a septoplasty in the same surgery, called a rhinoseptoplasty. At noon, it runs 2 to 3 hours under general anesthesia, inside Q2's licensed surgical suites. (Q2 is a dedicated surgical facility, separate from the office where you first meet your surgeon.) Which technique you need, and whether septoplasty should be added, gets decided by your surgeon at the in-person consultation, not a search engine.

    Downtime isn't the same as "no longer visible"

    Ten to fifteen days of downtime cover the most visible stretch of recovery: the nasal splint and the swelling around the eyes in week one, then the splint comes off and you ease back into light activity in week two. What those days don't cover is the finer swelling that keeps fading between months 1 and 3, or the tip of the nose, which keeps settling for close to a year. If you're working from Houston or New Jersey with your camera on every day, or you have a family event coming up, that changes how you plan the full first month, not just the two-week window your employer sees as downtime.

    That's not meant to talk you out of it. It's honesty about the pace of this particular surgery, which runs slower and more visibly than something like liposuction or breast augmentation, where clothing covers most of what's healing.

    What we won't promise you

    No surgery is risk-free, and a rhinoplasty, even though it doesn't touch the body, is no exception. We don't promise the exact nose from a saved photo, or a final result before the time it actually takes: up to 12 months for the tip to fully settle. We also won't tell you upfront whether you'll need nasal packing; that depends on your technique and your case, something your surgeon explains at the consultation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently asked questions

    How long does rhinoplasty take, and what anesthesia is used?

    At noon, it runs 2 to 3 hours under general anesthesia, always in an operating room, inside Q2's licensed surgical suites, never in a consultation office.

    How many days off work do I actually need?

    Approximate downtime is 10 to 15 days. You wear a nasal splint the first week, with swelling around the eyes, and it comes off in the second week, when you return to light activity.

    Will it still be noticeable once I'm back on camera for work?

    Most of the fine swelling fades between months 1 and 3, but the tip of the nose keeps settling for up to a year. If your job runs on video calls, plan around that first month, not just the 10 to 15 days of downtime.

    Can it be combined with septoplasty if I have trouble breathing?

    Yes, that's called a rhinoseptoplasty. If your septum is affecting your breathing, your surgeon evaluates at the consultation whether to correct it in the same surgery.

    How many days do I need to stay in Medellín?

    We use a reference of 1 to 2 weeks for face surgery, built to leave room for your first follow-up visit before you fly. Your surgeon confirms the exact date once you're in Medellín.
