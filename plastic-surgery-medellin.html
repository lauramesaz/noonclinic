    Plastic surgery

    Face

    Eyelids, nose, chin, ears and facial rejuvenation.

    • Eyelid SurgeryA more rested look: it treats excess skin and bags on the eyelids.1 to 2 hours · Time off work 7 to 10 days→
    • RhinoplastyBalance between your nose and the rest of your face, with your breathing in mind too.2 to 3 hours · Time off work 10 to 15 days→
    • Facelift and neck liftDeep rejuvenation that repositions the tissues of the face and neck.3 to 5 hours · Time off work 2 to 3 weeks→
