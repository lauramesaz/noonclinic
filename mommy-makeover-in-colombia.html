    Flying in for a mommy makeover

    Mommy Makeover in Colombia,
    for US Patients

    Combining breasts and abdomen into one surgery isn't a scheduling choice. It's your surgeon's call, and it changes how long you need away from home.

    Guides · noon Clinic team · Updated October 14, 2026

    Two pregnancies, a long stretch of breastfeeding, and what's left doesn't match what you had, and it doesn't respond to the gym either. You've been searching mommy makeover in Colombia, comparing what's available near Houston or Miami against what Medellín offers, and at some point you got to the real question: can breasts and abdomen actually happen in the same trip? The honest answer is "it depends," and the person who answers it is your surgeon, not a pre-built package.

    What a mommy makeover in Colombia actually is (and who decides if it's all one surgery)

    At noon, a mommy makeover combines procedures like a breast lift or breast augmentation with a tummy tuck and liposuction, in a single surgery, to help restore your body's shape after pregnancy. It isn't a fixed package: it's planned around each patient. It runs 4 to 6 hours under general anesthesia, inside Q2's licensed surgical suites, never in a consultation office. (Q2 is a dedicated surgical facility, separate from the office where you'll first meet your surgeon.)

    Here's the part that actually matters: doing everything in one surgery only happens when your health allows it and the resulting surgery time is reasonable. If not, it gets staged. Your surgeon makes that call with your pre-op testing in hand, at the in-person consultation, not from a flight calculator. If the decision is to stage it, that means two trips instead of one, each with its own downtime and its own follow-up visit before you fly home.

    3 to 4 weeks of downtime reshapes the trip

    This is the longest downtime among the body-contouring surgeries we offer, longer than a tummy tuck alone (2 to 3 weeks) and longer than a breast augmentation without a lift (1 week). That tracks: you're combining several areas in one surgical window. Our reference for staying in Medellín is 2 to 3 weeks, built to cover your first follow-up with the surgeon before you fly. The downtime itself keeps running once you're back home.

    For a mom living in New Jersey or Atlanta, that number isn't just clinic logistics. It's who watches your kids while you can't lift anything, how many days your job will actually give you, and whether someone can travel with you. You'll need a companion for the first few days; that isn't optional, it's part of recovering well from a surgery this size. Traveling with a companion for surgery abroad (also in Spanish) covers what that person does day to day, and how long to stay in Colombia after plastic surgery (also in Spanish) breaks down how that number gets built for different procedures.

    What we won't promise you

    We don't promise everything can be done in one surgery just because that makes the trip simpler. We also don't promise a result that matches a saved photo, or a body with "no trace" of pregnancy: the final result takes about 6 months to settle, with real steps along the way (relative rest the first week, compression garments and check-ins between weeks 2 and 4, exercise once your surgeon clears it between months 1 and 3). No procedure is risk-free, and combining several areas adds a bit more to manage than a single one does; that's why pre-op testing, and the decision to combine or stage, aren't paperwork, they're the real filter.

    What we can tell you: an in-person consultation with Dr. Fernando Ruiz, a plan that tells you what goes together and what doesn't, and follow-up by WhatsApp or video call once you're back in the US, which supports but never replaces the in-person checks you need before flying. If you're still weighing how to pick the right place for this, is it safe to get plastic surgery in Colombia (also in Spanish) covers that.

    Before you book the flight, message us on WhatsApp and tell us what changed in your body after your pregnancies, and how many days you actually have. With that, and your pre-op testing, we figure out whether your mommy makeover happens in one surgery or in stages, and how many actual trips to Medellín that means for you.

    Frequently asked questions

    Frequently asked questions

    What exactly is included in a mommy makeover?

    At noon, it combines procedures like a breast lift or breast augmentation with a tummy tuck and liposuction, in one surgery, to help restore your body's shape after pregnancy. Which of those you actually need, and whether they all go together, gets decided at your consultation.

    Is it safe to do breasts and abdomen on the same day?

    Only if your health allows it and the resulting surgery time is reasonable. If not, your surgeon splits it into stages. That call depends on your pre-op testing, not on whether you already booked the flight.

    How long does the surgery take, and how much downtime does it need?

    At noon, it runs 4 to 6 hours under general anesthesia, always in an operating room, inside Q2's licensed surgical suites. Approximate downtime is 3 to 4 weeks.

    Can I get pregnant after a mommy makeover?

    Physically, yes, but it isn't recommended. A later pregnancy can separate the repaired muscles again and affect the result. That's why not planning more pregnancies is part of being a good candidate.

    How many days do I need to stay in Medellín?

    We use a reference of 2 to 3 weeks for combined body-contouring surgeries, built to leave room for your first follow-up visits before you fly. Your surgeon confirms the exact date once you're in Medellín.
