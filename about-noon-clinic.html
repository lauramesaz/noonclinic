    Plastic surgery in Medellín, Colombia.

    A plastic surgery and aesthetic medicine clinic for patients from Colombia and abroad. WhatsApp +57 302 249 1937.

    Quick facts

    noon Clinic at a glance.

    • WhereMedellín, Antioquia, Colombia.
    • SpecialistsDr. Fernando Ruiz, plastic surgeon, and Dra. Valeria Enciso, aesthetic physician.
    • Where surgery happensIn licensed operating rooms (Q2), never in a doctor's office. Pre-op tests are mandatory.
    • First stepAn in-person consultation in Medellín with the specialist who would treat you. WhatsApp is for questions and booking, not for diagnosis.
    • Once you are back homeFollow-up by WhatsApp or video call. It does not replace the in-person check-ups your specialist sets before you fly.
    • PricesNot published online: you get a written plan and quote after your consultation.
