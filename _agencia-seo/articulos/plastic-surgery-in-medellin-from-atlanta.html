If you're considering plastic surgery in Medellin from Atlanta, here's the first thing to know: there is no nonstop flight between the two cities. That's not a dealbreaker. It just means your trip, and especially your flight home, needs more planning than a quick hop from Miami.

This page is for Atlanta residents weighing the trip: Colombian Americans with family back home, and people who simply keep hearing about Medellin and want the honest version.

Getting from Atlanta to Medellin

You have two realistic options:

  • Delta, Atlanta to Bogota, then a domestic flight to Medellin. Delta flies nonstop from ATL to Bogota, Colombia's capital. Short domestic flights run from Bogota to Medellin.
  • Connect inside the U.S. Miami has nonstop service to Medellin on American and Avianca (roughly 3 h 30 min). Houston has a United nonstop (roughly 4 h 40 min). Fly ATL to one of those hubs, then straight to Medellin.

Flight times are approximate and routes change, so check current schedules. One more thing travelers don't expect: Medellin's international airport, José María Córdova (MDE), sits in the town of Rionegro, outside the city. Plan for the drive to your hotel.

The flight home is the part to plan hardest

Flying down is easy. Flying back a week or two after surgery is different. A connecting itinerary means more hours in a seat, a walk through a terminal, maybe a security line.

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons notes that the risk of blood clots from immobility, long flights included, is higher in the six weeks after surgery. As a general reference, the ASPS suggests waiting 5 to 7 days before flying after body procedures and 7 to 10 days after facial surgery. At noon, that's background, not a rule: your surgeon clears your flight at your follow-up visit.

What that means for booking:

  • Pick a sensible layover. Too short and you're rushing between gates. Too long and you're sitting for hours.
  • Buy a flexible return ticket. Your return date depends on how you heal, not on the calendar.
  • Bring a companion. For surgery, you need an adult with you for the first days. On a two-leg trip home, they handle the bags and the connection.
  • Ask before discharge. Compression garments, moving during the flight, medication: your surgeon gives you instructions for your case.

Once you're home in Atlanta, you can still reach us. We follow up remotely by WhatsApp or video call. That doesn't replace the in-person checkups in Medellin before your flight, and in an emergency, go to a local emergency room.

How long to stay in Medellin

These are the reference ranges we give patients traveling from another city:

  • Face surgery: 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Breast surgery: 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Body contouring and combined procedures: 2 to 3 weeks. That covers liposuction, a tummy tuck or a mommy makeover.
  • Non-surgical aesthetic treatments: usually the same day or a few days.

If you're asking for PTO, ask for more than you think you need. Not everything fits into a one-week trip, and a combined procedure almost never does.

Atlanta's Colombian community

About 49,000 people of Colombian origin live in metro Atlanta, according to the Census Bureau. It's a smaller community than Miami's or New York's. If you have family in Colombia, you may want to combine the trip with a visit. That works, as long as the visit doesn't eat into your recovery. Surgery and follow-ups come first.
