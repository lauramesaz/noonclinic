What a mommy makeover in Colombia actually is (and who decides if it's all one surgery)

At noon, a mommy makeover combines procedures like a breast lift or breast augmentation with a tummy tuck and liposuction, in a single surgery, to help restore your body's shape after pregnancy. It isn't a fixed package: it's planned around each patient. It runs 4 to 6 hours under general anesthesia, inside Q2's licensed surgical suites, never in a consultation office. Here's the part that actually matters: doing everything in one surgery only happens when your health allows it and the resulting surgery time is reasonable. If not, it gets staged. What we won't promise you

We don't promise everything can be done in one surgery just because that makes the trip simpler.
