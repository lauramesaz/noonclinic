Questions

What people traveling
from abroad ask.

Do I need a visa?

With a US passport, you don't need a tourist visa and can stay up to 90 days. If you're also Colombian, you must enter and leave Colombia on your Colombian passport. Please check current requirements before you travel.

Can the estimate change?

Your surgeon confirms your plan at the in-person consultation. If something different or additional comes up there, you get the quote in writing before you decide.

How long will I stay in Medellín?

As long as your surgeon recommends. As a guide, one to two weeks for breast surgery and two to three for liposuction; your surgeon clears you to fly at a follow-up visit. More in our guide how long to stay in Colombia after plastic surgery.

Who follows up with me once I'm home?

Before you fly, you do your follow-ups in Medellín. Once you're home, we stay in touch by WhatsApp or video call. If you need to be seen in person, see a local doctor and let your surgeon know; for an emergency, go to the nearest ER.

Can I travel alone?

For surgery, you'll need an adult with you during the first days. Their travel costs aren't included in the price.

Can I pay from abroad?

Message us and we'll walk you through the payment options. You pay in Colombian pesos.

What if my flight changes?

Ask to change your date in advance, on WhatsApp. The new date depends on operating room availability and, for a year-end package, it has to be before January 15. Buy flexible flights and lodging: if surgery is postponed, travel costs aren't refunded.

What if I pay the deposit and change my mind?

If you pay the COP 1,000,000 deposit and then decide not to have surgery, we refund COP 800,000. The consultation fee isn't refundable. Full details are in our terms and conditions (in Spanish).
