    Why noon

    What you can check.

    We don't ask you to take our word for it. These are the rules we work by, and how to check any clinic before you decide.

    How we work

    Ten reasons, no adjectives.

    • 01The same specialist, from consultation to dischargeThe plastic surgeon who sees you at your consultation is the one who operates and follows you until you are discharged.
    • 02Surgery only in licensed operating roomsWe operate in the licensed operating rooms of Q2. Never in a doctor's office.
    • 03Always an in-person consultationNo one has surgery or treatment without being examined in person. WhatsApp is for questions and booking, not for diagnosis.
    • 04Mandatory pre-op testsIf your test results are not in order, surgery is postponed. No exceptions.
    • 05Your plan and quote, in writingProcedure, timing, aftercare and cost, clear from the start.
    • 06Every risk, one by oneBefore you decide, we go over the risks with you and you sign an informed consent form.
    • 07We know when to say noIf a procedure is not safe or does not make sense for you, we tell you.
    • 08Plastic surgery and aesthetic medicine under one roofA plastic surgeon and an aesthetic physician: if a non-surgical option suits you better, we suggest it.
    • 09Information backed by official sourcesEvery procedure page cites the American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS), the FDA or the CDC, and says who it is not for.
    • 10Follow-up from a distance, tooCheck-ups until discharge. If you travel from another city or country, we stay in touch afterward by WhatsApp or video call.

    Before you decide

    Check any clinic.

    You have the right to confirm that your surgeon is registered as a specialist and that the facility where you have surgery is licensed. Colombia's Ministry of Health keeps public registries (in Spanish):

    • ReTHUS ↗Registry of doctors and specialists.
    • REPS ↗Registry of licensed clinics and surgical facilities.
